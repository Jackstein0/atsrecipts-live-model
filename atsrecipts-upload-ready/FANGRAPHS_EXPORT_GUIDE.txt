FanGraphs Export Guide
======================

The tool can now use FanGraphs CSV exports.

Double-click:
Setup FanGraphs Exports.command

Then replace the template files in data/fangraphs with FanGraphs exports:

1. data/fangraphs/team_batting.csv
   Required columns:
   - Team
   - wRC+

2. data/fangraphs/starters.csv
   Required columns:
   - Name
   - xFIP
   Helpful column:
   - MLBAMID

3. data/fangraphs/bullpen.csv
   Required columns:
   - Team
   - xFIP

The importer accepts common variations like lowercase names and wRC_plus.

After those files are present, Run Today.command overlays FanGraphs exports on
top of the MLB proxy data automatically.
